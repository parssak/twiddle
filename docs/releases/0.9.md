# Twiddle 0.9

- Adds a focused first-launch setup for system audio, optional top-row shortcuts, and Open at Login. Existing installs keep their choices, and setup can be reopened from Settings.
- Keeps Settings at the normal window level so it no longer stays above other apps.
- Makes trackpad scrolling on the filter knob follow the same direction as dragging.
- Reduces idle energy use when audio and effects are inactive.
